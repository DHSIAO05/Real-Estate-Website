About Jennifer

A San Jose local who treats your move like her own.

My husband and I have called San Jose home for 28+ years, raising our three boys here. All three played high school baseball, and one went on to play for a Pac-12 college team. I've seen firsthand why the Bay Area is consistently ranked among the best places to live in America: exceptional people, work opportunities, neighborhoods, schools, and diverse activities.

I have a passion for real estate and bring 20+ years of experience in customer support and negotiation at high-tech companies. I strive to help every client find their dream home.
