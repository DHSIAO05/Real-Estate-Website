About Jennifer

A Bay Area local who treats your move like her own.

Jennifer has called the Bay Area home for more than 28 years. She knows firsthand why the region is consistently ranked among the best places to live in America, with its strong job market, top-rated schools, and diverse neighborhoods. That local insight helps her clients find not just the right house, but the right community.

Before real estate, Jennifer spent over 20 years in customer support and negotiation at leading high-tech companies. There she learned to listen closely, solve problems quickly, and advocate firmly. Today she brings those same skills to every transaction, guiding buyers and sellers with clear communication and a steady focus on the best possible outcome.

“I am here to listen, understand your needs, and go above and beyond to deliver exceptional results. Together, let’s make your real estate dreams come true.”
